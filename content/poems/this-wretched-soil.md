this wretched soil

among rivers profane, he searches high and low,
for a dosage of rain, that frees him from limbo,
the tall trees deny fate, wandering depths below,
enthralled is the wise sage by wherever the wind blows,

the initiate ponders the black sun and red moon,
this land of perdition where the demons yet loom,
a lone soul moribund, a revelation's postscript
his voice a tone high strung, he deciphers the crypt

it yields forbidden fruit, a staff of mercury,
hidden under dry soot, in lands of perjury,
his frail corpus transmutes, from gold to arsenic,
the story now concludes, with fate's hands choleric,

he looks upon his work, with his skin violet,
the sage hides behind murk, and remains inviolate
