a matter of context

a memory remembered persists through the cosmos
a dream forgotten ripples into one's awakening
a puzzle that resists the solutions only god knows
some stories are best left without an ending

concealed are these frayed synapses
the psyche reveals, eclipses, and passes
the key to the great beyond collapses
under the weight of what a tesseract is

refractions of questions posed since antiquity
contractions of answers recycled in mixed quality
an adjourned testimony like roses without thorns
a verdict that strikes like the most violent of storms

when the heart and feather have been balanced
would there be further use of material talents
where the rivers soar and the firmament flows;
a flagrancy unknown to this earthen globe?

secrets sewn into sequential materiality
underlying an interweaving lattice of the surreal
whose patterns give rise to a complex musicality
that bends intuition on the nature of what’s truly real

the grand expanse modeled in mandelbrots and fibonaccis
patterns sheer that they melt the brain like liquid gold
so ingrained is our fascination and perplexities
that spirits high started communicating in italics and bold

the intricate internal logic of a black box
dreams resurfacing within commercial blocks
the albino raven’s yin, a black swan’s yang
in the midst of a tempest, a raft a melody sang

what is hidden beyond what the plane corporeal
can lay claim and name before time immemorial
the flash of insight that dissolves into oblivion
its traces of first light more ancient than vermillion

as we survey the sun from core to corona
we have yet to pinpoint one single persona
of the life sustaining iota in the sky
so too can something so bright also lie

beneath every truth lies a deception
behind every deception is a kernel of truth
a revelation revered, the true conception
for other narratives there is no more room

mystery enshrouded by smoke and mirrors
a story enshrined by its most devout believers
to the dreamers and seekers, symbols are clearer
to the pragmatists, the concern is what’s nearer

can a rose resurrect hope for a better tomorrow,
or does the carnation reignite the souls borrowed?
the trails we tread recorded in many prints
but the laughter and tears the trail cannot evince

and should the heavens send down a savior
to judge human nature and all our behavior
i hope that the judgment considers context
of the stories left behind that continue to collect
