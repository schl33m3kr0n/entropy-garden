war

when the petals of violence bloom
& the sky inherits the fog
rivers of the heart leave the room
their deltas flooded in smog

the body a weather forecast
of thunderous downpours 
that the roots of plains vast
dine on for the next course

parables adopt paranoia
monoliths earn monograms
symbols precede amnesia
mom & pops become grams

words turn into projectiles
cradles become craters
deceit looks like wide smiles
process pretends to be greater

an empire built on displacement
a melting pot of expressions
we still don’t know what he meant
by his rabbling intercessions

hiding in the shade
where the light doesn’t hit
history is always made
behind doors closed & unlit
