a distant reality

Through all the static and noise
Your echoes ring the clearest
The sweet lilac of your voice
Like a dream i hold dearest

The summers & winters pass
My silence becomes louder
The thoughts long withheld amass
With a passing of each hour

My heart gathers each meeting
And replays them as a song
The mask I wear retreating
Revealing how much I've longed

Spry mornings fade to late nights
Buried under memories
Of what could have been had I
Crossed a distant reality
