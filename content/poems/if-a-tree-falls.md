if a tree falls

'twas the heart that was torn in twain
victim to many scorns unnamed

what's freedom to the vagabond
but an excuse to sever bonds

was he doomed to isolation
by the burden of his station

when once the land was bucolic
and the fields were game to frolic

the illusion of connection
was simply exaggeration

should this one revolving chamber
ease the return to the manger
