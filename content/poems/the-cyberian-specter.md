the cyberian specter

the bricks we build for babylon
bespoke in leafless birches
what we offer to the amazon
hides in heedless lurches

data the new scripture of the aeon
trading rosaries for digital service
seeking out a new eden beyond
while the machine sifts and searches

i fell into the sky and the ground dissolved
the blood in my veins turned to chrome
and poured into the crucible unsolved
solemnity a spectacle under the dome
