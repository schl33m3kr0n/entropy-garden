the seeker

he rises at sunset
he rests in the sunrise
in his hand a goblet
brimming with the stars’ light
many a legend sel-
dom speaks his common name
among his company
few true friends can he claim

yearning for truths beyond
he pores through scriptures old
composing many songs
his tunes mercurial gold
his conclusions fragment
branching into fractals
a long search of lament
seeking out the capsule

his charts and diagrams
layered over tenfold
ciphers and anagrams
yet no story can be told
to what great ends can ex-
istence itself extend
into oblivion
these grand metaphors blend

in the mountain's oppress-
ion he is engulfed
his mind under compression
his lungs full of sulph
he searches and searches
but the soul conflagrates
he cursed a litany
and left through his old gates

so he ventured forward 
under crystal moonlight
the chaos of order 
abandoned with morbid spite
with wings wide spread he dove
to depths of the unknown
into a lonely cove
lit by a small faint glow

there he met providence
forever shapeshifting
of unknown provenance
through sand he was sifting
he asked the man’s business
no words were uttered
he looked at the stillness
the man’s corpus fluttered

and after a brief pause
he no longer lamented
he rises at sunrise
and rests in the sunset
