finding north

the numeral of the bellowing beast below
the tetragram of the one almighty above
the river east of the garden of eden flows
west to the remainder of what is and once was

a geometry tempered by endless fire
whose symmetry frames one’s outlook
just as creation can raise itself higher
it can collapse with a sweep of the foot

the solitary simplicity of the one called unity
the universe collapsed to a single identity
the creative impulse of converting none to one
treating as the same the moon, earth, and sun

the all or none, the double edge of duality
the source of opposition and polarity
from the shell of the self to that of the other
it is through difference that we discover

the troubling paradox of the trinity
the minimum prerequisite for stability
but also where our predictions quit
incarnations joined yet somehow split

the ordered mayhem of multiplicity
the challenge and rub of reality
thence we travail under the ire of ironies
the wicked and the wise in a single anomaly

a reality of seemingly infinite layers
making sense of multiple sensations
ask for the sight of the soothsayers
they’ll ask if it will change the destination

where the leviathan meets the behemoth
casting shadows over land or water
where the seraphim sit at the zenith
in their vision the lamb before the slaughter

the sclera of the sequential sky cerulean
the sun, the ever so radiant iris
an eternal witness to matters vitruvian
until the heart is weighed before osiris

as we orient around providence
and his litany of covenants,
should our matters of substance
pin sacraments against fundaments?

our eyes peering into the heavens
as they look back at us behind the mist
our centuries of research and discretion
to their aeons of patience and gnosis

our timeline is a single path
one wrought of grace and wrath
yet beneath the compass glass
a familiar cycle comes to pass

between material and metaphysical
emerge questions modern yet biblical
these values of fables and vectors
a mosaic of tones and textures

hundreds of millions of years of evolution
tint the colors of our intention and vision
our second natures projecting value and direction
on things without weight or dimension

laying the groundwork for what could be
trimming the options based on what should be
pouring the concrete to bolster the concept
giving proportions to what was a precept

a cycle known to us that rinses and repeats
without a safe haven from the storm to contemplate
until this grand game of life completes
whose many rulebooks we still consecrate

to man, everything must possess
a name, a story, and a value
and from these possessions egress
identity, relations, and if fortunate, a statue

to divest such a foundation
is to deface, alienate, and erase
but the soul is the instantiation
of what can never be misplaced

the legacy that we leave behind
our pride and pains in summation
the tempest crosses the borderline
it does not bow to any nation

the tempest never asks which direction
it only travels where the air flows
no middleman in the connection
finding north doesn’t require prose
