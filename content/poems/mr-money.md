mr money

while 1 in 3 people are stuck living paycheck to paycheck
mr money owns a pool, a maybach, and a mansion with 8 beds
you have bounced payments and second hand raiments
he just spent a whole deposit on some customized marble pavement

you’re stuck living your life in the cage of the wage slave
he has two bengal tigers that he calls “rage” and “brave”
you live in a food desert and look over twice your age
he has an onsite chef, a chauffeur, and a lifted face

you do what you can to maximize your chances
when he buys some stocks, the market chart dances
you create art pieces to spark a conversation
he buys blue squares to avoid tiered taxation

you make your peace with the help of a pastor
in the back of his mind, he thinks he’s the master
all your life you were told to hate handouts
his dad said that to avoid paying higher amounts

you work overtime on top of 2 more jobs
he worries about which speedster takes which keyfob
you pray to god for a way to escape the rat race
he asks if his attorney on retainer is the right race

you play it safe to avoid high medical bills
he goes skydiving every week for a view that kills
you work half a lifetime to pay off all your loans
he likes to throw parties until he’s all skin and bones

you waste half your check on a traffic citation
he gets a slap on the wrist for the same violation
you have your heart set on the perfect girl
he looks at her and says “let’s give it a whirl”

you can’t afford to dress casual or fail a drug test
he wears polo while snorting coke off a woman’s chest
you wonder if you’ll ever have to take from your 401k
he makes the same amount and then some in less than a day

you dream of owning a matte-finish convertible
he owns cars that can go sideways and vertical
you spend your life keeping up with the joneses
he uses a map of his house to find where his phone is

you wear clothes that cost less than a dime a dozen
he buys luxury two-pieces for his second cousin
you worry if this is the month you become homeless
he prefers his gourmet rotisserie chicken boneless

you work in hopes of making more than triple digits
he multiplies commas in less than a New York minute
you never left the borders of your own city limits
he visits the Canary Islands dressed as Mr. Peanut

you shed a tear every time you buy gas or groceries
his bank account is arranged like a rosary
