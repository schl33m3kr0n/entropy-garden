a matter of when

what’s the compensation for alienation
to assume a station for one’s pigmentation
seeking through the confusion, finding the illusion
of the conclusions of crowns buried deep in delusion

when the leader steps up to the podium
to repeat their talking points ad nauseam
all they’re missing is their balloon animals
the real work goes to low wage practicals

when the stairs collect more steps
with each scare of an insufficient check
when you can barely afford to pay rent
and weigh the pros and cons of living in a tent

see how far we’ve come in making progress
as we bet fortunes on a loaded congress
that implore us still to trust in the process
while the machine rusts expecting us to acquiesce

the outcomes on which we gamble is striking, bar none
on who’ll have won the war that’s only just begun
or how the weather will be when we hit DEFCON 1
a guarantee that the forecast predicts a second sun

the singularity we’ve built on our backs
yearning for civilization’s eventual collapse
waiting for humanity’s age to elapse
we can only seal so many structural cracks

when public opinion doesn’t affect public policy
we’re just puppets and victims without an apology
of elites with their own private fleets
who are given audience by the highest of seats

when private interests intersect with civic duty
what does it mean when the end of an office is more booty?
god forbid someone exist and at the same time be fruity
“not in front of the kids” they say while leaders hide their cruelty

when the law puts some people at an advantage
should they sulk when paying for the bandage?
the burden to manage, to minimize damage
to the profits of those with the highest point of vantage

catastrophes and tragedies, symptoms of the system
what does liberty look like when imprisoned from wisdom
when certainty of absolutes comes under question
closely observe those who scoff at the mere suggestion
