an unfolding

an altar to an ambler
another aimless ascetic
at once altered and amber
afflicted by an ailment iambic

a beauty unbeknownst to brooks
bliss borne of birches of flame
brittle yet breathtaking in books
bestowing badges of what became

the crimson crescent’s crescendo
a craft of creation crystallizing
a curse, a cry, an incredible credo
crested in choruses canalizing

an existential ecstasy of experience
the echoes of an excavation
inexorable to experts ever since
the explosion and expansion

youth yields to years of yonder
yet the eye yearns for yeses
yellowed is the yarn of yore
Yahweh yells no ingresses

zen or zeal zipped and zeroed
a zephyr among zoloft zealots
ziggurats and Zion’s zipcode
a zigzag of rezoned secrets
