the eternahedron

what is left of an incarnation
who has seen everything
whose rights hail from a far nation
but here there is not a thing?

out of days dark and nights light
is the destiny of creation
a sun hidden and a moon in sight
are they to end in destruction?


within these walls rests the master
a prisoner of his own making
without, the slave toils faster
the warden against awaking

without words a scar cannot heal
as the harms of the past congeal
within the future, only confusion
the present a simple illusion
