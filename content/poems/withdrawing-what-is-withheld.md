withdrawing what is withheld

when the weeping and wallowing from woe awakens from waist to wrist
a wanton wave of the wan willow, a worsening wound the more we watch
would it be wise to well up the weakness or wheel it away to wherever?
would we worship the worst of our ways because the weakness wields an iron will?
or would we worship the winter whittling away at our wants and wishes?
when we wish for what was, we witness a war within
without a warden against our woes, watching what we were warned about
west of wisdom do we walk when the wave of what once was washes in
at wit’s end, the wake of wonder whisked off to the wide world
we were not willing to witness the wolf in our woods
nor did we wish to witness the wings that may wrench us from our own wreckage
we walk away from the weeping willow to the wisdom of the walnut
when we wander where the wind whistles and whispers
the world washes your worries away, the water we wade in wistfully wanes the words
our window to withdraw awaits, a fair wage for what we’ve withheld
and the walls may widen once again where we may finally move forward
