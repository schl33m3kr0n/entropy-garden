the cosmic casino

between polarities sits the lattice
expanding locally into infinite depths
the duel of dualities an impetus
for the prime thread until death

a story preceding the primordial
what was nothing was still something
a state of matter beyond corporeal
tainted with the prospect of becoming

a cycle, the prime’s foundation
in a festival of perpetual recursion
the metric most minuscule in relation
to a refinement making conversions

underlying the foundation, the tesseract
of space and time’s insolence
fields that converge and counteract
against a lonely universal innocence

the theory, an eternal transmutation
revolving, revealing its faces
a forever journey of implication
of a truth without traces

an eagle of the abyss
from an evolving vantage point
too many factors exist
for the house to count all its coins
