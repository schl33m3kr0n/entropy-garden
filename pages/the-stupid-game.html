THE STUPID GAME

Roll the die to start Round 1.
🎲 R1 0c POT: 0c
  • Goal: 6 players, 6 secret colors (R, Y, B, G, K, W). Start with 3c each. Last survivor wins.
  • Die Roll: Each round, a 1–6 die roll gives every survivor that many credits.
  • Guess (1/round, Free): Click a player chip and pick a color dot. A right guess exposes their color & piece.
  • Move (1c/step) & Kill (3c): Tap any board square to move. Stand 1 orthogonal space (N/S/E/W) from an exposed target to kill them for 3c.
  • The Pot: Dead players' credits + anonymous donations enter the Communal Pot.
  • Bounty (2c) · Trial (10c Pot) · Revive (15c Pot): Put a +2c bounty on the selected player (paid to their killer), spend 10c from the Pot for a majority-vote execution anywhere, or spend 15c from the Pot to revive a dead player with 3c.
