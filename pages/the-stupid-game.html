THE STUPID GAME

six players. six secret colors. one survivor. murder costs 3 credits; anonymous generosity costs infinity.

1. Pieces & Secret Colors

6 players each receive 3 starting credits on a Check and one secret color (Red, Yellow, Blue, Green, Black, White). All pieces appear by color on the 8×8 grid; who controls which color is secret until identified.

2. Die Roll & Credit Store

Each round begins with a 1d6 roll: every survivor earns the die roll in credits. Move costs 1 CR per orthogonal step. Anonymous donations fund the Communal Pot (or cost ∞ CR for pure philanthropy).

3. Guessing & 1-Space Assassination

Each player gets 1 free color guess per round. A correct guess reveals that player's identity. You may assassinate a revealed target for 3 CR only if you end movement 1 orthogonal space away (N/S/E/W — no diagonals).

4. Communal Pot, Trials & Resurrection

Eliminated players' remaining credits enter the Communal Pot. The pot can fund a People's Tribunal (10 Pot CR) — majority vote eliminates any accused player anywhere on the board — or a Resurrection (15 Pot CR), reviving an eliminated player with their original color and 3 CR.
